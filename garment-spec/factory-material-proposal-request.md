## What JACKETEK has decided

- **Body shell: Cotton Fabric** (design intent).
- **Sleeves: Leather** (design intent). Please propose the leather. Synthetic / PU may be offered only as a clearly labelled **ALTERNATIVE SUPPLIER PROPOSAL — NOT CURRENT JACKETEK DESIGN INTENT**, with its cost, MOQ, performance and appearance differences. It does not replace Leather without JACKETEK's explicit approval.
- Varsity **knitted rib** collar, cuffs and waistband, matching, **striped colourway supported**.
- **Metal snap** front closure, in four finishes: black, silver, gold, gunmetal.
- **Lining required** (body and sleeves).
- Two front angled **welt pockets**. Welt material: **FACTORY TO PROPOSE / JACKETEK TO APPROVE** (X-04 open); the drawings show the welts in the sleeve material — a visual reference / option only.
- **Dropped shoulder** (K1). Measurements: see the M sample validation plan.
- Embroidery in fixed zones: 4 on the cotton body, 6 on the leather sleeves.
